# Bin day

Which bins go out, the evening before and on the day itself: "Tomorrow · PMD, Paper and cardboard". On other days the
widget hides and frees its place. Read from your municipality's waste calendar as an iCal link, every six hours.

## Setting it up

Find the calendar link your municipality or waste company publishes (in Belgium e.g. Recycle!, in the Netherlands
the municipality's afvalkalender; look for "add to calendar", "iCal" or ".ics") and paste the `https://` address.

The plugin reads all-day events: the event's title is what goes out ("PMD", "Restafval"), several on one day are
joined with a comma.

## What it may do

- **Network:** only the address you type in. Nothing else.
